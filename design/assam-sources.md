## Fiscal calibration

All fiscal amounts in the model are nominal lakh rupees. The opening treasury is ₹49 lakh, the rounded result of applying the existing 32% playable share to the ₹154 lakh closing cash reported for 31 March 1947. The source lists ₹102 lakh separately in reserve funds. The turn budget applies the shared 0.5% playable share of revenue receipts, subject to the existing development-spending cap where figures are available. Taxes shared by the Centre and revenue grants inform the split between local funds and Central transfers. Negotiated grants in play use the existing simulation rules. [Constituent Assembly, Expert Committee on Financial Provisions, Annexure III, Appendix B, p. 80](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/762996/1/cad_04-11-1948.pdf)

The national *Provincial Development Programme* gives Assam development expenditure of ₹43, ₹122, ₹172 and ₹239 lakh for FY 1946–47 through FY 1949–50. Central grants financed about 84% of that four-year development programme. The percentage covers development spending specifically. The revenue-transfer calculation uses the available whole-budget transfer breakdown. When a fiscal year lacks a receipt figure, the game carries forward the latest source for the same profile and displays the fiscal year it used. The FY 1946–49 opening period uses total receipts and the local-funding default where the game has no complete Central transfer breakdown. [Government of India, *Provincial Development Programme*, chapter III, Tables I–II](https://dspace.gipe.ac.in/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10973/39679/GIPE-022067.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3)
