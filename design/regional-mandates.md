# Regional mandates through 1985

From 1974 through 1979, the Cabinet in a long founding campaign can choose one regional mandate. It gives the ending a definition of success the player can choose before commissioning later works. The same decision works with both politics presets. A mandate is optional, costs nothing to sign, and stays fixed once chosen. Players still pay normal costs for the works and services they use to reach it.

The timing follows the major boundary settlements in the playable campaigns, including Assam's move to Dispur in 1973. The Planning Commission's draft Fifth Plan considered development through 1985–86. That period frames this chapter. The three mandates and their numerical targets are game design. [Planning Commission draft Fifth Plan](https://digitallibrary.niti.gov.in/items/7704c183-de3f-4a44-a0df-80c0d4eb0da1)

| Mandate | Outcome | Facility operating in 1985 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food resilience | Four years between 1981 and 1985 with a harvest that meets demand, zero shortage, and closing reserves of at least half the year's demand rounded up. Agricultural extension and Food & Water priorities must add or recover at least three food units combined in each qualifying year. | Regional water network |
| A trained workforce | Four new cohorts graduate from the mandate's signing year onward and enter service by 1985. Each cohort needs five funded teaching years at its campus and enters service the following year. | Engineering or agricultural research institute |
| An industrial region | Four years between 1981 and 1985 with workshop output at least 150% of the administration's existing long campaign output goal, rounded up. At least six output each year must come from direct trained workforce and production priority gains. The output target is set on signing. | Industrial park |

Earlier works can satisfy the facility requirement if they are still operating under the player's administration. The outcome requirements give the player a reason to keep developing a portfolio. They allow different routes. Agricultural extension can support a food mandate while a five-year priority serves another department. Earlier education programmes and cohorts can help qualify industrial years while the industrial park is in delivery. Starting Education early gives a buffer for slower campus openings or an existing priority term.

The Cabinet keeps the mandate accessible from the map after the offer is dismissed. The comparison uses the department colours and icons from the trees. After signing, the mandate opens its associated tree directly. The annual brief displays the count, facility status and the result for the current year. Archived briefs preserve their original count. The ending uses the chosen mandate as its headline and keeps the two original campaign goals below it. Saves without a mandate keep their existing ending.

## Balance evidence

A matched engine comparison used public political, construction, grant and priority actions with seed 1946. It branched from the same 1974 position within each of 14 administrations across the nine starts, including Northern Bihar, both Mayurbhanj settlements and both western, northern and southern successors. All three focused strategies met their mandate in each administration. All matched normal service runs without the relevant farm support or workforce gains fell short, even where their completed works had already met the signature project requirement. Each branch could reinvest in new programmes using its own treasury.

In the production browser, a Standard Bihar campaign completed 40 years using the Cabinet, Trade & Industry tree and annual brief. The 1981 report counted the first qualifying year at 106 output against a target of 66, with 18 direct output from production services. The ending scored five qualifying years and an operating industrial park. Reloading after choosing preserved the mandate, and the archived 1974 result retained its original progress.

These thresholds have one matched seed across the roster. Further evaluation should compare other monsoons, later signing, different early portfolios and the clarity of the new choices with human players. The mandate measures model outputs and does not change cash flow. The [late campaign finance review](late-campaign-balance.md) tracks the outstanding nominal price and receipt work.
