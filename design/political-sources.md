# Founding campaign history

Historical milestones anchor the campaign’s dates. Cabinet terms, project prices, resource effects and alternative outcomes are simulation choices.

- **1947 independence and Partition.** The Indian Independence Act established the dominions of India and Pakistan on 15 August 1947. Bengal was divided between them. The regional relief packages and development priorities are modeled. [Indian Independence Act, section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Geo6/10-11/30/section/1/enacted) · [West Bengal Legislative Assembly history](https://lalib.wb.gov.in/showOverview) · [Ministry of Home Affairs on rehabilitation](https://www.mha.gov.in/MHA1/Par2017/pdfs/par2016-pdfs/rs-160316-2214.pdf)
- **1949 Mayurbhanj merger.** The ruler signed the merger agreement on 17 October 1948. The Dominion took over on 9 November, and Orissa assumed administration on 1 January 1949. A compact that leaves two works offices is an alternate outcome. [Government of India White Paper, page 46](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3AWhite_Paper_on_Indian_States_%281950%29.pdf/51)
- **1950 Constitution.** Article 394 brought most of the Constitution into force on 26 January. Articles 275 and 280 provide for grants and Finance Commission recommendations. The campaign's immediate allocations and projects represent state implementation choices. [Constitution of India](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/88cca69e868e50b217f855be2fb8bdba.pdf)
- **1956 Bihar.** The Bihar and West Bengal transfer took effect on 1 November. The southern petition uses the 1956 boundary debate as its setting. The council, southern split and quarter-share transfer are alternate outcomes. [Bihar and West Bengal (Transfer of Territories) Act, sections 2–3](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1636/1/195640.pdf)
- **1966 and 1972.** India launched the High Yielding Variety Programme in 1966. The severe 1972–73 drought raised pressures on food supply and prices. The campaign models local seed adoption and grain relief choices. [ICAR agricultural history, section 2.1.6](https://www.icar.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-12/Indian-Agriculture-after-Independence.pdf) · [Planning Commission Fifth Five Year Plan](https://digitallibrary.niti.gov.in/items/4e710904-80f7-475c-b34c-e697ab98a726)
