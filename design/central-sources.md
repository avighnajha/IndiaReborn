# Central Provinces and Berar fiscal sources

All amounts are nominal rupees. The opening treasury uses 32% of the ₹241 lakh closing cash balance for Central Provinces and Berar on 31 March 1947, rounded to ₹77 lakh. The expert committee lists this cash balance separately from reserve funds and from its ten-year revenue totals. The game uses the closing balance as an opening benchmark. [Constituent Assembly, Expert Committee on Financial Provisions, provincial balances table](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/762996/1/cad_04-11-1948.pdf)

The Ministry of Finance records Central Provinces development outlays of ₹116 lakh in FY 1946–47, ₹256 lakh in FY 1947–48, ₹814 lakh in the revised estimate for FY 1948–49 and ₹1,171 lakh in the FY 1949–50 budget. It distinguishes development grants and loans from provincial revenue surpluses, loans and cash balances. The game uses the common playable budget of 0.5% of total revenue, capped at 30% of the historic development outlay where available. The Budget Office gives the date and basis of each reference. Years with a gap in the series carry forward the latest available reference for the same fiscal profile. [Ministry of Finance, *Provincial Development Programme*, chapter VI, tables I and II](https://dspace.gipe.ac.in/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10973/39679/GIPE-022067.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3)

The intermediate receipts come from Finance Commission tables through FY 1963–64 and from state accounts reproduced in *State Finances in India* for FY 1964–65 to FY 1977–78. Using the later state data avoids carrying the Fourth Finance Commission’s estimates for FY 1964–65 and FY 1965–66 into the game. [Fourth Finance Commission, appendix VII, table 20](https://fincomindia.nic.in/asset/doc/commission-reports/4th-FC/digitized/4th%20FC-Digitised.pdf#page=197), [*State Finances in India*, volume 2, chapter 2, table 1](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/320291468752761432/pdf/multi0page.pdf#page=41)
