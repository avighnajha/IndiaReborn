# Second Plan development partnerships

Bihar and Orissa can prepare two bids for one Union-supported regional works package. The Cabinet shows the requirements from 1953. It can commit one state share between 1957 and 1960. The Union pays its recorded contribution directly to the works. The ordinary programme queue handles the three years of delivery, which competes with other construction. The alternative closes on signing. The standard department tiers and general development grants remain available.

The Planning Commission's Second Plan review covered both river basin works and industrial expansion. At Hirakud in 1957, Nehru described irrigation, flood moderation and power as parts of the same regional development problem. His 1956 note on technical manpower emphasized planning across several Plan periods because training takes years. These sources inform the context. The four packages, application window, matching shares and facilities are authored alternate-history choices. The food package opens the store first. The embankment and first farm open in year two, and the second farm follows in year three. The depot adds capacity for 18 food units to the state's reserve. The farm sites use the regular weather and protection rules. The trade package opens the college and first workshop in year two, then the second workshop in year three. The college adds 6 Skills and raises output at linked workshops; the new suppliers use the ordinary market, workforce and power rules. In Standard, linked suppliers increase freight bid capacity as they open.

The two plans use the normal programme preview and familiar facility icons. The relevant Department tree shows the partnership below its four tiers. All routes used the same political and weather conditions within their regional comparison. In both regions an unchosen branch was also completed.

Both directions were also reached and completed on seed 1947 under Light politics, including a Northern Bihar split. In the Standard Bihar comparison the suppliers put the 1961 freight bid at 5, up from 3 without the award. This meets the minimum; rivals still compete for the award. The Food & Water comparison grew closing reserves and retained fields but the tested common portfolios already prevented shortages by the late years. Food supply pressure and the original victory thresholds still need review when comparing larger portfolios. [Late campaign balance review](late-campaign-balance.md)

In a second comparison, every branch took the first affordable ordinary programme after the award year, whenever the works office was free. Bihar finished with 71 food in reserve and 94 workshop output on the reserve route, and with 53 food and 128 output on the supplier route. Orissa finished at 71 and 96 on the reserve route, and 53 and 134 on the supplier route. The no-award runs had 53 in reserve and output of 112 in Bihar and 96 in Orissa. The investments still changed the final economies when later construction continued. All six runs reached both original victory goals, which strengthens the case for reviewing the scorecard and late resource pressure.

## What the ending shows

The founding scorecard compares the opening and final five years of the administration. Food reserve coverage divides closing stores by that year's food demand and expresses the ratio in months. Skills and workshop output are annual averages. Jurisdictions can change during the campaign, so the two periods describe the player's administration at each end of the journey. Actual paid Union freight terms, linked workshops, public food shipments and teaching results appear when the reports record them. Partnership production at the project sites has its own dated entry and a map link.

The industrial goal now requires the regional output and market link targets in the same year in at least four of the final five years. Treasury remains in the budget summary. The shared prosperity goal and optional regional mandate still use their existing rules. Archive scenarios retain their original industrial threshold. Completed founding saves use the new assessment when opened, based on their saved reports.
