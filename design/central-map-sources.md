# Central Provinces and Berar Map Sources

| Campaign phase | Source and coverage |
| --- | --- |
| 1946 opening | The 1941 census layer uses the 19 British districts of Central Provinces and Berar. The gold outline excludes the surrounding princely states. Nagpur is the seat. |
| 1948 integration | The 1951 Madhya Pradesh outline represents the 14 Eastern States that joined on 1 January 1948 and Makrai, which joined on 1 February. The playable map represents those territories at campaign scale around Jagdalpur, Kanker, Raigarh, Surguja and the other town anchors. |
| 1950 republic | The 1951 Madhya Pradesh outline remains in use after the change of name under the Constitution. The seat stays in Nagpur. |
| 1956 reorganization | The 1961 Madhya Pradesh outline approximates the new state from 1 November 1956. It includes Madhya Bharat, Vindhya Pradesh, Bhopal and Sironj. The eight Vidarbha districts pass to Bombay, and Bhopal becomes the seat. At the scale of this map the 1961 outline includes any minor changes made before the census. |

The 1948 dates follow the Government of India’s White Paper on Indian States, paragraphs 72–74 and the table of states merged into provinces. The 1956 transfers follow sections 8 and 9 of the States Reorganisation Act, including the Sunel exclusion and the Sironj transfer. [Government of India White Paper](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a8/White_Paper_on_Indian_States_%281948%29.pdf) · [States Reorganisation Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1680/1/a1956-37.pdf) · [History of Bhopal](https://bhopal.nic.in/en/history/) · [High Court of Madhya Pradesh](https://portal.mphc.gov.in/history-constitution)

India State Stories digitized the census outlines used in this local build. Neighboring lines also use the contemporary census layers. The relief combines Mapzen Terrarium elevation with Natural Earth land, rivers and reservoir geometry. The national game uses the separate atlas at its own map scale. See the [production map notes](map-production.md) for input hashes and redistribution status. [India State Stories](https://www.indiastatestory.in/datadownloads) · [Historical census mirror](https://github.com/ramSeraph/indian_admin_boundaries/releases/tag/historical) · [Mapzen terrain](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/) · [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/)

Settlement coordinates approximate town centres. The Hoshangabad marker is on the town’s south side to keep the Narmada-bank settlement inside the 1951 outline at game scale. Gandhi Sagar marks the project on the Madhya Pradesh side of the reservoir. The reservoir reference enters the map from 1960, its historical year of impoundment. Bhilai appears from 1955 with the steel project. The Bhilai choice uses the first furnace opening in 1959. The plan links distinct farm and transport sites for play, with permanent site IDs and modeled ownership. [Census of India, Jabalpur handbook](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/29200) · [Durg district geography](https://durg.gov.in/en/about-district/) · [Central Water Commission, Gandhi Sagar](https://cwc.gov.in/sites/default/files/final-report-gandhisagar2015.pdf) · [Parliamentary Estimates Committee, Bhilai](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/57981/1/ec_05_20_1971.pdf)

Rebuild with `python tools/geography/build_geography.py INPUT_DIR GAME_DIR --region central`. Use the 1941, 1951 and 1961 historical census input files and the Natural Earth and Terrarium layers described in the production map notes.
