# Five-year cabinet priorities

Implemented 23 November 2026. This regional operating choice, available in founding campaigns from 1951, continues departments after construction. Existing services and buildings keep their baseline effects when no extra priority is chosen.

India's First Five Year Plan covered 1951–56. The [Planning Commission summary in NITI's library](https://digitallibrary.niti.gov.in/items/a9861f42-e35d-483f-b3f0-afffed09b8f6) provides period context. The game's rolling terms, choices, funding rate and effects are authored mechanics, not a reconstruction of a historical Plan.

## Terms and money

Choose Education, Food & Water, Trade & Industry, or Normal services from **Five-year priority** in the Cabinet or Development trees. Cards share department colours and icons, show the current annual rupee bill and use two short effect bullets.

- One focus runs for five calendar years. Other focuses and Normal services cannot replace it during that term. There is no automatic renewal. A shorter final term can end with the campaign; Education requires a cohort that can enter service before the campaign ends.
- The modeled annual appropriation is 30% of that year's works allocation, rounded to lakh with a one-lakh minimum. The rupee bill can change with the fiscal reference or governed administration. This is disclosed before commitment; it is not a fixed-price construction contract or an observed historical operating share.
- Charge the bill once during resolution, after reserving an operating teaching charter's upkeep and before emergency food purchases. Adoption takes no cash. The programme panel warns if construction would leave an otherwise fundable focus paused.
- Insufficient opening cash or no eligible facilities pauses that year's focus at zero cost. Buildings and teaching progress are retained. The calendar term does not extend.
- Forecasts and resolution use the same works due to open that year. Education campuses must already have completed a year; a campus cannot train a full cohort on its opening turn.

## Three different priorities

| Priority | Funded service | Continuing result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Education | Each owned, mature education campus accumulates a funded teaching year. Several institutions at one hex count as one campus. | Every five funded teaching years produces a cohort. From the following year, each cohort adds 2 Skills and 1 direct output per market-linked workshop. Its Skills also follow the normal Skills calculation. |
| Food & Water | Each operating irrigated area adds 1 food and can recover up to 2 remaining weather losses, after existing defences, extension and water networks. | Preserves supply and reserves through adverse seasons. Extra services end with funding. |
| Trade & Industry | Each linked workshop adds 3 output. Receipts follow the existing 80% output rule. | Earns earlier receipts; the bonus stops when funding ends. It does not add freight bid or route capacity. |

Education progress attaches to physical campuses. A campus outside the player's administration stops contributing to the player's Skills and workforce. Its record remains. Supported successor AI exchanges inherit a modeled contribution through their existing works account: inherited workshop count × inherited cohorts in extra output, with 80% rounded down credited as receipts. Local and distant settlements post that income once. AI exchanges otherwise retain their independent development rules; they do not use the new player controls.

Teaching charters remain separate: they assign established teams to apprenticeships or extension. Five-year Education funding trains the broader future workforce. Existing charter terms are preserved.

## Presentation and review

The annual brief shows actual spending, the current priority's production or food result, and earlier workforce output separately. Graduation names the next service year. The detailed ledger separates priority spending from works and charter upkeep. Earlier reports are not reconstructed.

**Locate priority sites** opens Economy view from the latest report. Department-coloured rings and a dated legend identify served facilities. Workshop inspection separates Skills, trained workforce and production priority. Campus inspection shows completed cohorts and funded years toward the next. Archived briefs keep their recorded results without offering a current-map priority overlay.

Matched public-action Bihar runs distinguish timing: Food removes two adverse-year shortages; Trade adds output only while funded; Education produces later and persists without renewal. Checks reconcile cash, charter and purchase reservations, previews and deterministic resume. A production browser campaign exercises all three focuses, graduation, later terms, expiry, map inspection and reload through 1985 at desktop and narrow widths.

The [late campaign review](late-campaign-balance.md) remains open. Large portfolios, period prices, competing AI priorities, later objectives and human playtime still need review. These rates and benefits are candidate values; neither the checks nor source grounding establish enjoyable, balanced play.

A separate paid public-action Punjab campaign funded Education before the actual 1966 choice to govern Haryana. Nine trained cohorts divided by physical campus: four remained with Haryana and five passed to Punjab. Haryana's workforce bonus and Punjab's inherited workshop receipts used their respective shares, and the regional and national reports agreed. This verifies the successor path as well as the synthetic ownership boundary check.
