## Delivered foundation

- Player and AI use the same later revenue observations for the same administration. More precise regional observations win when both sources cover the same year. Earlier jurisdictions never acquire a successor's budget automatically.
- New player reports save the actual allocation, historical source, source year, transfer assumption and workshop/market receipts. Earlier report amounts use their recorded figures; missing source details stay missing.
- The annual brief distinguishes the public allocation and its change from earned receipts. The budget source can be expanded in the detailed ledger and Economy breakdown.
- Optional [five-year priorities](cabinet-priorities.md) now add recurring departmental claims alongside construction. Food has an immediate resilience role, Trade a temporary production benefit, and Education a delayed, enduring workforce. The brief separates current priority effects from older training, and Economy view identifies beneficiaries.
- [Regional mandates](regional-mandates.md) give 1985 campaigns a player-selected ending goal after boundaries settle. The targets use measured service outcomes and a final tier facility. Matched focused strategies meet all three mandates across 14 tested administrations. Normal services alone miss the targets in the matched runs.
- [Period prices](period-prices.md) now apply to new works, ordinary receipts and upkeep. Quotes record their price year and preserve earlier paid commitments. The brief shows the portion of the change in workshop and market receipts due to prices.

For example, Bihar's modeled envelope moves from ₹60 lakh using the older reference to ₹494 lakh using FY1980–81 receipts. This is a change of available historical reference. It is not measured GDP growth, a player achievement, or a reconstruction of every intervening budget.

## The 1981 bulk freight award

The 1981–85 Standard campaign award opens for review in 1978. Earlier awards and saved terms keep their rules. Link three market towns, a workshop and a freight terminal, then fund Trade & Industry in the award year. A linked industrial park improves the offer within the network's physical capacity. The strongest eligible bid wins and equal bids rotate. Nearby rivals need an operating exchange or inherited terminal, a loading yard and an operating processing plant or inherited workshops. Their bids use their existing route and handling limits.

The award commits six existing freight slots each year. The holder earns ₹150 lakh when all six loads ship, at a fixed nominal fee set using the existing period-price index. The modeled fee starts at ₹4 lakh per load in 1952 prices and rounds to ₹25 lakh per load at award. Emergency claims are deducted first. Contract loads use capacity before commercial orders. The award stays with the same holder if annual conditions fail. Payment and capacity use resume when the holder can deliver. A Trade term begun in 1981 spans all five years. A shorter remaining term must be renewed to deliver through 1985.

The economic survey describes difficulty moving bulk freight after 1977–78. A Parliamentary report documents the period's preferential railway traffic categories and emergency exceptions. The tender, capacity, fee and relationship to state services are campaign rules. [Economic Survey 1981–82, infrastructure](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/budget_archive/es1981-82/3%20Infrastructure.pdf) · [Estimates Committee report on essential commodities transport, April 1981](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/759230/1/ec_07_10_1980.pdf)

An independent public-action comparison measured seven funding schedules over seven matched setups. In the prepared Bihar run, Trade carried 30 bulk loads and earned ₹750 lakh gross, using six of thirteen slots each year. It carried ten further spot loads across five years. With Education, West Bengal carried the bulk loads, Bihar carried 24 spot loads, and Bihar produced 154 workshop output in 1984 and 1985 against 130 under Trade. The Trade branch closed ₹265 lakh ahead of Education and ₹26 lakh ahead of ordinary services. Haryana lost the modeled bid to Punjab's larger inherited network. Earlier Trade terms interrupted delivery if left unfunded at expiry. Light omitted the tender. These runs establish a service trade-off under the tested portfolios. Human assessment, the late Food path and a wider set of AI industrial strategies remain open. The existing relief windows occur in the 1960s. A focused check exercises relief priority in the freight allocation rules.

Two installed Standard Bihar playthroughs followed the same seven-programme portfolio from 1946. Trade carried all 30 bulk loads and recorded 106 output in 1985. Education left the award with West Bengal and reached 112 output after its cohorts began work. The browser review checked the 1978 notice, 1981 autosave reload, opening the terminal on the Economy map, and the freight card at desktop and 430 px. The lighter portfolio also missed food security in every final year, a useful comparison against the reserve-sufficient portfolios in the automated review.

### Price series for implementation

The RBI archival table publishes annual wholesale prices from FY 1952–53 through the campaign's ending and provides overlapping rows at each change of base. The all-commodity column can be linked by using 125.1 for FY 1961–62 on the 1952–53 base, 181.1 for FY 1970–71 on the 1961–62 base, and 281.3 for FY 1981–82 on the 1970–71 base. This yields a broad historical price scale. The table warns that commodity groups change composition between bases. Use the separate Economic Survey FY 1950–51 observation as early context and document the assumption for the years before the annual series.
