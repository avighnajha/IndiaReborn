# Late campaign finance and strategic choices

Reviewed 23 November 2026. This is an open production balance issue, separate from whether the historical revenue observations are correct.

## What the review found

A delegated reviewer completed all nine Standard 1946–1985 campaigns using public engine actions: choose the first affordable political option and commission the first affordable visible programme. No cash or assets were added. This is a scale probe, not an optimal strategy or evidence of enjoyable human pacing.

## Delivered foundation

- Player and AI use the same later revenue observations for the same administration. More precise regional observations win when both sources cover the same year. Earlier jurisdictions never acquire a successor's budget automatically.
- New player reports save the actual allocation, historical source, source year, transfer assumption and workshop/market receipts. Earlier report amounts use their recorded figures; missing source details stay missing.
- The annual brief distinguishes the public allocation and its change from earned receipts. The budget source can be expanded in the detailed ledger and Economy breakdown.

For example, Bihar's modeled envelope moves from ₹60 lakh using the older reference to ₹494 lakh using FY1980–81 receipts. This is a change of available historical reference. It is not measured GDP growth, a player achievement, or a reconstruction of every intervening budget.

## Next implementation sequence

1. **Complete nominal cashflow rules.** Review newly quoted construction prices and operating receipts together. RBI's [annual wholesale-price series](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=17173) is a possible documented proxy, not a construction cost index. Respect changes in the series base. Later-authored programmes need their own price year; existing payments, signed contributions and training terms remain fixed. Any indexed trade must settle the same payment in both treasuries. Saved monetary receipts now make it possible to change future rules without rewriting old reports.
2. **Continuing policy commitments.** Develop a higher-level operating budget choice that gives Education, Food & Water and Trade & Industry continuing claims after construction. Compare continuing education and research funding against food resilience and industry rather than treating completed institutions as permanent free output. Specify benefits, service failure and reversal before implementing; retain clear map and brief feedback. This answers the user's request for sustained choices within each department.
3. **Later objectives and pacing.** Review cash and prosperity goals by administration and duration, and keep food security, output and actual earned receipts distinct. Compare matched policy paths across small, large and successor administrations. Do not raise prices solely to consume a surplus or claim that inflation creates strategic depth.
4. **Human review.** Measure whether a new player can explain what they gave up by choosing a department, trace the effect in the brief and map, and find a worthwhile choice in later years. Keep the existing national coverage, art, accessibility, performance and historical release gates open.

Prices, outputs, portfolio shares and upkeep remain authored game values in the current build. The shared revenue update improves provenance; late campaign balance remains unapproved.
