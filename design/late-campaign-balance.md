# Late campaign finance and strategic choices

Reviewed 23 November 2026. This is an open production balance issue, separate from whether the historical revenue observations are correct.

## What the review found

A delegated reviewer completed all nine Standard 1946–1985 campaigns using public engine actions: choose the first affordable political option and commission the first affordable visible programme. No cash or assets were added. This is a scale probe, not an optimal strategy or evidence of enjoyable human pacing.

## Delivered foundation

- Player and AI use the same later revenue observations for the same administration. More precise regional observations win when both sources cover the same year. Earlier jurisdictions never acquire a successor's budget automatically.
- New player reports save the actual allocation, historical source, source year, transfer assumption and workshop/market receipts. Earlier report amounts use their recorded figures; missing source details stay missing.
- The annual brief distinguishes the public allocation and its change from earned receipts. The budget source can be expanded in the detailed ledger and Economy breakdown.
- Optional [five-year priorities](cabinet-priorities.md) now add recurring departmental claims alongside construction. Food has an immediate resilience role, Trade a temporary production benefit, and Education a delayed, enduring workforce. The brief separates current priority effects from older training, and Economy view identifies beneficiaries.
- [Regional mandates](regional-mandates.md) give 1985 campaigns a player-selected ending goal after boundaries settle. The targets use measured service outcomes and a final tier facility. Matched focused strategies meet all three mandates across 14 tested administrations. Normal services alone miss the targets in the matched runs.
- [Period prices](period-prices.md) now apply to new works, ordinary receipts and upkeep. Quotes record their price year and preserve earlier paid commitments. The brief shows the portion of the change in workshop and market receipts due to prices.

For example, Bihar's modeled envelope moves from ₹60 lakh using the older reference to ₹494 lakh using FY1980–81 receipts. This is a change of available historical reference. It is not measured GDP growth, a player achievement, or a reconstruction of every intervening budget.

## Next implementation sequence

1. **Complete nominal cashflow rules.** Construction quotes and ordinary operations now use the linked wholesale price series. Define explicit unit prices and conserve buyer and seller payments before repricing food trade. Review freight and procurement terms separately. Compare treasury and upkeep at consistent prices when evaluating cash surplus.
2. **Continuing policy balance.** Five-year priorities are implemented with explicit terms, annual costs, pauses and campus-bound education progress. Matched runs demonstrate different timing and food outcomes. Review large portfolios and later nominal budgets, teach the distinction from construction and charters, and consider matching rival policy commitments. The 30% rate and benefits remain candidate values; some small workshop portfolios do not recoup the Trade bill through receipts alone.
3. **Later objectives and pacing.** Regional mandates are implemented. Compare second and later seeds, late signing, unlocked portfolios, and alternative ways of meeting each mandate. Review the original cash and prosperity goals by administration and duration. Keep food security, output and actual earned receipts distinct when calibrating targets.
4. **Human review.** Measure whether a new player can explain what they gave up by choosing a department, trace the effect in the brief and map, and find a worthwhile choice in later years. Keep the existing national coverage, art, accessibility, performance and historical release gates open.

Prices, outputs, portfolio shares and upkeep remain authored game values in the current build. The shared revenue update improves provenance; late campaign balance remains unapproved.

### Price series for implementation

The RBI archival table publishes annual wholesale prices from FY 1952–53 through the campaign's ending and provides overlapping rows at each change of base. The all-commodity column can be linked by using 125.1 for FY 1961–62 on the 1952–53 base, 181.1 for FY 1970–71 on the 1961–62 base, and 281.3 for FY 1981–82 on the 1970–71 base. This yields a broad historical price scale. The table warns that commodity groups change composition between bases. Use the separate Economic Survey FY 1950–51 observation as early context and document the assumption for the years before the annual series. [RBI archival annual table](https://www.rbi.org.in/SCRIPTs/PublicationsView.aspx?id=8241) · [Economic Survey 1994–95, Table 0.1](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/budget_archive/es1994-95/9%20Social%20Sectors.pdf)

Cross-government food trade will need explicit prices on both sides of each shipment. Before indexing AI receipts, separate cash income from operating indicators that affect freight capacity. Budget references, accumulated cash, signed commitments and archived balances retain their original nominal values.
