# National campaign coverage

Reviewed 22 November 2026 against the [original handoff, V0.7–V1.0](India_Alternate_Futures_Codex_Handoff.md#v09--india-wide-campaign-beta). The candidate beta roster contains nine authored 1946 starts. This inventory records the remaining work toward national coverage; it does not close the national beta or release gates.

## What is simulated

**Playable** means a founding campaign with programmes, political choices, fiscal references, a regional map, saves and an ending. **Market** means an administration has its own harvest, reserves, money and investments under Standard politics. **Territory** means a place is included in ownership changes or historical geography, without an independent annual economy. These categories overlap: a playable region uses the full campaign model when selected and can be a market in another campaign. Light politics retains nearby trade and agreements and omits the national clearing network.

The atlas's **Beyond your borders** panel previews the actual market graph by year. In a resolved Domestic market, it lists only the accounts in that saved report. The number changes with the year and selected government. Nearby bilateral actors do not all belong to the domestic graph. A market point represents an exchange, not sovereignty over a drawn area. Census outlines are dated references rather than exact current-turn administrative boundaries. Public source builds continue to omit the historical outline layer while redistribution rights are unresolved.

## Northeastern market continuation

| Account | Annual entry and route | Historical basis and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manipur · Imphal | Own account from 1950; Assam road capacity one food unit per year | Indian administration began 15 October 1949. The 1961 Census handbook describes the Imphal–Dimapur road during the 1944 siege and as an operational land connection in 1961. Choosing 1950 uses the first full annual turn and does not claim a railway to Imphal. [Manipur Public Service Commission](https://www.mpscmanipur.gov.in/historical-perspective.html), [1961 Census handbook](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/28576/download/31758/22872_1961_MAN.pdf) |
| Tripura · Agartala | Own account from 1950; no domestic land route through 1954; Assam road capacity one from 1955 | Indian administration began 15 October 1949. Assam Assembly's 14 June 1957 record says the metalled road was completed in December 1954 and handed over in January 1955. This model does not simulate earlier air supply or cross-border routes. [Tripura High Court](https://thc.nic.in/history.html), [Assembly record](https://aladigitallibrary.in/bitstream/123456789/1878/11/ALA_Debates_The_14th_June_1957.pdf), [1960–61 Commission report](https://tribal.gov.in/downloads/other-important-reports/Dhebhar-Commission-Report-01.pdf) |
| Arunachal Pradesh · Bomdila | Separate account and one-unit Assam road from 1972 | The 1971 Act created a Union territory in January 1972. The road existed earlier: July 1960 BRDB minutes discuss Foothills–Bomdilla road maintenance and Assam links. Entry in 1972 marks a separate modeled administration, not road construction or statehood. Bomdila is a market hub, not a claim about the territorial capital. Statehood in 1987 lies outside the campaign. [1971 Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/1534?view_type=browse), [1960 meeting minutes](https://nehruarchive.in/documents/at-the-border-roads-development-board-16-july-1960-1p6og), [West Kameng district history](https://westkameng.nic.in/history/) |

Manipur and Tripura retain their existing accounts and works when they become states in 1972. Their balances are not reset. Each road clears at most one food unit in either direction in a year, with the existing food priority, reserve floor and buyer cash constraints. Households consume delivered relief; other deliveries enter stocks. Each delivered unit transfers money between the two governments. All three administrations can develop through the existing rival investment rules. Added markets appear on the next resolved turn of an older save; archived reports remain unchanged.

Harvests, food units, road capacities, AI development envelopes and investment prices are gameplay values. These small market accounts are not representations of a state's complete historical budget. The separately sourced playable-state fiscal model continues to govern player allocations. Further fiscal calibration for these new actors remains part of national balance review.

## Remaining national beta work

1. Add sourced northern connections for Himachal and a clearly scoped Jammu market. Review historical control and avoid implying that a Jammu exchange simulates all disputed territory.
2. Review a period-appropriate Sikkim trade model, distinguishing the kingdom/protectorate from its 1975 entry as an Indian state.
3. Add coastal enclaves and island shipping with their actual administration and access dates. Goa remains a Union territory within this campaign; statehood is in 1987. Pondicherry has Indian administration from 1954 and de jure transfer in 1962. [Goa Assembly](https://www.goavidhansabha.gov.in/infrastructure_detail.php?i_id=6), [Puducherry government](https://www.py.gov.in/node/1780), [Lakshadweep history](https://lakshadweep.gov.in/about-lakshadweep/history/)
4. Review whether aggregated princely administrations need separate pre-integration economies. Do not infer completed simulation from the territorial integration events.
5. Review the candidate roster and multiple victory paths through fresh player sessions. Measure campaign pace, supported-browser performance, keyboard flows and save recovery. Resolve map licensing and historical review before release.

The original roughly one-to-four-hour human playtime goal cannot be established by automated turn advancement. Browser runs and financial checks provide evidence for stability, rather than evidence that the full game is fun or release-ready.

## Review path

1. Open the Campaign atlas, choose Standard and expand **Beyond your borders**. Change Market preview from 1946 to 1972. Manipur, Tripura and Arunachal appear in the later market list without changing the campaign date or save. Switch to Light to see the narrower scope.
2. Begin Assam, Standard, through 1985. Fund Food in 1946, Trade in 1948, Rail Link Feeders in 1950, Technical Schools in 1953, the Union-backed Brahmaputra agreement in 1956 and Industry in 1959. Resolve the historical chapters normally.
3. Check Neighbors after 1950, 1955 and 1972. Imphal, Agartala and Bomdila become direct partners at the recorded dates. Each road shows its one-unit annual food limit. Agreements and depots preserve that limit. Reload after the 1972 reorganization and continue through the ending.
4. In a Standard central campaign, resolve through 1972 and open Domestic market. The expanded northeastern schematic separates the seven market labels. Active and idle road routes identify their type and one-unit capacity. Saved-report coverage lists only that year's accounts. Review at desktop and narrow widths.

The focused checks in `check-national-coverage.cjs`, included in `npm test`, cover dates, cash and food conservation, direct and domestic account transitions, treaty and shared-reserve caps, freight bids, descriptions and deterministic resume. Installed production Chrome completed the Assam and central review paths. Human pacing, northern and maritime coverage, map rights and the remaining release gates are still open.
