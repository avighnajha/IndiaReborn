# Northern market connections

The northern continuation adds market administrations under Standard politics. It retains the nine playable founding starts. A market hub is an exchange location, rather than a representation of an entire sovereign territory.

Account entry years and exact capacities are game abstractions. The Himachal connection groups limited hill routes; it does not claim that a particular Mandi highway opened in 1948. No automatic full-year closure is invented for the 1965 or 1971 wars. Jammu's marker does not simulate all Jammu and Kashmir, assert control over disputed territory, or introduce new territorial outlines. The coverage panel retains the territory beyond the Jammu exchange as outside the market model.

## Local and domestic routing

In a Punjab continuation, Mandi and Jammu appear in Neighbors at their entry dates. Each route's emergency releases, purchases and sales share its annual food limit. Spot freight and contract forecasts respect the same capacity scale. A depot, training school or treaty cannot bypass the route limit. The larger Jammu rail route can carry more food and freight after 1972 without creating money or supplies.

After choosing Haryana, these exchanges remain in the domestic graph through Punjab. They do not gain a fictitious direct Haryana–Jammu link. A West Bengal continuation gains Gangtok as a local partner in 1975. In other campaigns, these administrations use the domestic routing and competition rules. Local accounts are excluded from a second annual resolution, preserving carried money, reserves and investments.

Physical works transferred to Himachal in 1966 remain at their sites. Operating workshops earn the receiving administration's modeled receipts, irrigation and storage contribute locally, and inherited upkeep is charged. This also applies when Himachal becomes distant after a Haryana choice. The Punjab Reorganisation Act's 7.78% cash allocation to the Union remains separate from Himachal's market account; the model does not invent a subsequent appropriation. ## Review path

Play Punjab with Standard politics through 1985, keeping Punjab in 1966. Compare Neighbors in 1972 and 1973: Jammu changes from a one-unit road to a three-unit rail route. Reload after the change. In Domestic market, the northern schematic separates Mandi, Jammu, Ludhiana, Ambala and Delhi. In a long West Bengal campaign, Gangtok enters in 1975. Compare the recorded budgets in 1979 and 1980, then inspect a historical reference in Reserves and treasuries. Short and long campaign rules, both successor choices, save continuity and the budget identities remain part of `npm test`.
