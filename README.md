# India: Alternate Futures

> This source build includes the campaigns, terrain, settlements and rivers. The digitized historical boundaries are held locally while redistribution rights are verified. The atlas, map and territory previews identify the unavailable outlines. See the [production map notes](design/map-production.md).

## Play the founding campaign

Double-click **Play India.cmd**. The **Campaign atlas** offers nine 1946 starts, including Assam in the northeast and Central Provinces and Berar. Choose Light or Standard politics and a short or long campaign. The shorter Punjab campaign ends in 1970 so it includes the 1966 division. Other short campaigns end in 1965 and long campaigns end in 1985. The production build opens at `http://127.0.0.1:4173`. Keep the launcher window open while playing. The build runs offline with Node.js and a WebGL-capable browser.

Each founding start autosaves separately at that local address. Reopen the launcher in the same browser profile and select the region to resume. Earlier Bihar and Orissa saves appear as separate archive choices. Starting over in a region asks for confirmation before replacing its founding save.

Use the **Regional programmes** agenda to preview a programme's footprint and commit its budget. The works office delivers one programme at a time and places the individual projects. The treasury and programme costs are in lakh rupees. The **Budget office** shows the historical revenue behind your development allocation and opens Union funding negotiations in 1952, 1957 and 1962. Select a hex to inspect it. Zoom in and drag to pan.

New campaigns open with a visual cabinet briefing. **First turns** guides you from funding a programme to reading your first annual report. **How to play → Cabinet briefing** reopens the briefing in an existing save. In **Development trees**, each icon identifies the same type of work across campaigns. Select a card for its local name, footprint and terms.

Advance through 1947 to choose a development priority and a separate response to Partition displacement. In 1949, Mayurbhanj and Orissa can integrate or agree to maintain separate works offices. Bihar can face a southern reorganization in 1956 if the plateau received less than one third of investment starts. The border choices show the proposed outline and explain who receives works and funds. **Chronicle** records choices alongside historical census snapshots. **Neighbors** opens treaty offers. The annual brief reports trade, migration, freight revenue and political decisions.

From 1953, two schools that have each taught for a full year unlock a technical school charter. In the cabinet sidebar, use **Compare specializations** to preview sites and choose Industrial Apprenticeships or Agricultural Extension. You commit to one charter for the rest of the campaign. Each has setup and annual costs, and the annual brief records its effects after service begins. Neighbors spend their own funds on reserve depots and freight exchanges. Check **Neighbors** for delivery dates and the effects on trade.

**Advance year** plays the monsoon and highlights delivered works before the report. **See report** skips the animation. The **Monsoon** layer recalls the last resolved conditions. Use **Emergency food plan** for a one-year purchase cap. Some programmes can finish a year sooner with extra funding.

The annual brief shows food security, treasury, earned receipts and major investment outcomes, including food or revenue from jointly funded neighbor projects. Open **Why this happened** for the harvest, workshop and treasury breakdowns. Use **Earlier** and **Later** to review past years. The final scorecard compares your results with each target and links to the final brief and chronicle.

Open the **Economy** map layer to find operating industry, schools, food and water facilities, and freight terminals. Zoom in for additional roles at the same site, or use **Jump to operating site**. After a workshop completes a year, **Locate workshops** in the latest brief opens the map. Select **Details** to see the site's recorded workshop output and what contributed, including market access, Skills and research. Irrigated sites show their measured harvest contribution. Older saves gain site production figures as new years resolve.

Under Standard politics, open **Domestic market** from **Neighbors** or any resolved annual brief. The map shows food shipments between governments and how routes change with Partition and state reorganization. From 1957, shipments through your neighbors can add freight orders. Inland exchanges can compete for those orders through nearby markets and reinvest the proceeds. [Interregional model and sources](design/interregional-model.md)

The **Neighbors** button flags the next freight contract during planning. Compare the capacity of your approved works with the strongest known rival. From 1961, a qualified winner can earn ₹6 lakh each year for five years. [Contract rules](design/interregional-model.md#regional-freight-contracts)

In 1956, check the **Union food reserve** notice in the Cabinet. With a market-connected food store and at least two linked towns, you can sign in 1957 to ship surplus at ₹2 lakh per unit through 1960. Choose between a larger shipment allowance and keeping more food at home. The forecast shows how each choice affects your reserves if the monsoon worsens. From 1958, eligible states can receive up to three units when their own supplies fall short. The annual brief records the shipment, payment, or relief. [Reserve rules and historical sources](design/interregional-model.md#union-food-reserve)

Open **Emergency food plan** in the Cabinet to see how the expected harvest and reserves would cover food needs under typical conditions and the current weather warning. The latest flood report opens the **Monsoon** layer and highlights the growing sites that lost food or benefited from protection.

- **Bihar** opens with ₹34 lakh. The Ganga Market Corridor commits ₹32 lakh to a crossing north of Patna, a link to Darbhanga and a workshop. Northern Food Security costs ₹29 lakh and improves the first harvest. Once it finishes, compare three **Kosi Flood Compact** agreements.
- **Orissa** opens with ₹19 lakh. Develop coastal and interior markets, fund schools, or start with **Delta Food Security**. The Mahanadi programme offers earlier, state-funded delta protection and a wider, Union-backed **Hirakud** agreement. To qualify for Hirakud, fund the 1948 survey. When the station opens, choose whether its power supports workshops or irrigation. Both effects appear in the annual brief.
- **Mayurbhanj** opens with a modeled ₹8 lakh. Choose tank and grain investment, Baripada market roads, or technical schools. Keep a local works office or join Orissa and take on a larger population, budget, and programme catalogue.
- **West Bengal** opens with a modeled ₹44 lakh. Develop the Hooghly port and mills, Bardhaman food reserves, or technical schools. Once Partition disrupts the jute supply, reopen an eastern trade route or invest in domestic cultivation. The 1956 settlement offers a choice between incorporating Purulia and the northern corridor or negotiating transit. [West Bengal campaign sources](design/west-bengal-sources.md)
- **Bombay** opens with a modeled ₹80 lakh. Develop the port and mills, invest in river works, and expand district services. In 1960 you can continue as Maharashtra or Gujarat with the works each state inherits and its own budget. [Bombay campaign sources](design/bombay-sources.md)
- **Punjab** opens with a modeled ₹57 lakh. Choose a response to Partition displacement, move the capital to Chandigarh and integrate PEPSU. Fund Bhakra–Nangal canal distribution or bring industrial power online first. In 1966, continue as Punjab or Haryana, inheriting the works inside your state. Both states keep Chandigarh as their administrative seat and share power from operating Bhakra works. [Punjab campaign sources](design/punjab-sources.md)
- **Madras** opens with ₹51 lakh based on its 1946 budget. Develop food, trade and technical schools on both sides of the province. In 1953, choose Madras or Andhra State and keep the works on your side. Andhra can develop the Krishna delta or the interior, while Madras can prioritize Cauvery canals or Neyveli power. Each has its own programmes, budget and 1956 territorial changes. [Madras and Andhra sources](design/madras-sources.md)
- **Assam** opens with a modeled ₹49 lakh. Keep supplies moving after Partition, restore market access with rail feeders, and choose between Brahmaputra flood protection and river freight. Govern through the Naga Hills transfer and Nagaland statehood. The long campaign includes Meghalaya and Mizoram and the move from Shillong to Dispur. [Assam campaign sources](design/assam-sources.md)
- **Central Provinces and Berar** opens with a modeled ₹77 lakh. Weigh the early returns from Berar cotton against works that stay in Madhya Pradesh. In 1956, move to Bhopal and decide how to keep the Nagpur trade route open. Then choose between Chambal irrigation and industrial power and decide whether to support Bhilai suppliers. [Campaign chronology](design/central-chronology.md)

## Stack and scope

The game runs on PixiJS 8 and WebGL for the interactive map, with a TypeScript renderer, Vite builds, and browser controls. Campaign rules run independently of the renderer. `index.html` is the browser entry point. The Windows launcher serves the compiled game from `dist` over the local loopback interface.

The regional maps show continuous shaded relief, geographically placed settlements and major rivers. **Borders** emphasizes the governed territory and the effects of major settlements. Inspect a town for approximate elevation. The nationwide atlas provides historical context. Annual simulation covers the selected region, its trading partners and a network of domestic food markets under Standard politics. The extended campaign carries forward the latest fiscal references. See the [production map notes](design/map-production.md), [political sources](design/political-sources.md) and [interregional model](design/interregional-model.md).

See the [project record](PROJECT.md) for current priorities, feedback and assignments, and [historical fiscal references](design/fiscal-sources.md) for source figures and the assumptions used to turn them into playable budgets. The original Word design and [Markdown handoff](design/India_Alternate_Futures_Codex_Handoff.md) describe the wider campaign roadmap.

## Build and verify

1. `npm ci` installs the locked development packages.
2. `npm run build` type-checks the renderer and produces the production build.
3. `npm test` checks the simulation, including both victory paths, deterministic resume, and project continuity when borders change.
4. `npm run dev` starts a development session. Use the launcher after rebuilding for production playthroughs, screenshots, and visual checks.
